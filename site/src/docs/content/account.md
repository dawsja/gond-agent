# Account and sign-in

Sign in with email and password, or with the identity provider configured by your administrator. Every account has every feature.

## Sessions

Settings lists your active browser sessions. Revoke any you do not recognize. Sign out ends your current session.

## OpenID Connect

Administrators configure OIDC in Settings. Use an HTTPS issuer URL with discovery support, a client ID and a client secret. The secret is encrypted locally and never returned by the settings API. Password sign-in stays available. Sign in again before changing authentication settings if your session is no longer recent.
